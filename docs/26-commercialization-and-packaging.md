# Commercialization & Packaging Strategy

**Status:** Hypotheses only. This document does not authorize an external launch.

## Why plan for productization
Internal use may reveal reusable workflows. Clean interfaces and exports reduce future migration cost, but do not justify building a full SaaS before demand is demonstrated.

## Packaging hypotheses
1. Internal intelligence workbench for HOLBERY — initial priority.
2. Evidence-backed research dossiers or recurring briefs, only where source rights permit.
3. Paid operator workspace for sources, evidence, opportunity comparisons and experiments.
4. Vertical intelligence package if repeated use proves a vertical-specific workflow improves outcomes.

## Value proposition to validate
Reduce time and uncertainty between observing a market problem and making a defensible product decision, with source traceability and explicit counter-evidence. Do not promise guaranteed sales, revenue prediction, full internet coverage or certainty about willingness to pay.

## Packaging variables
Approved connector breadth; collection frequency and retention; workspaces/collaborators; dossier/export limits; model usage; experiment tracking; audit/governance; support commitments.

Pricing must follow customer value, provider cost, support burden, source rights and observed willingness to pay. No prices are set here.

## Gates
A. Internal utility: repeatable workflow improves HOLBERY decisions.  
B. Evidence quality: provenance, deduplication, counter-evidence and limitations are reliable.  
C. External workflow validation: a target user completes it without constant founder intervention.  
D. Willingness to pay: paid pilot or real payment; compliments and survey intent are not revenue.  
E. Operational readiness: tenant isolation, export/deletion, incident handling, support and economics.  
F. Launch authorization: founder approves offer, market, price, claims, distribution, budget and scope.

Track qualified interest, pilot commitments, invoices, confirmed payments, refunds, usage, retention and realized value separately. Never report projected value as revenue.

Avoid subscriptions, self-serve billing, enterprise administration and many vertical-specific features until a repeatable paying use case is demonstrated.
