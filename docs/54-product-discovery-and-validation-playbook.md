# 54 — Product Discovery and Validation Playbook

Goal: find a defensible business product rather than a generic AI workspace. Use Genspark official materials, G2 reviews/categories, competitor docs, and accessible public user discussions.

## Research question

Which repeated business job is painful and expensive enough that an operator will pay HIO to complete it reliably, with better evidence and control than the current toolchain?

## Competitive lenses

- Genspark: persistent context, multi-step agents, work suites, collaboration, finished outputs.
- ClickUp/Asana: work management, tasks, team workflows, AI-assisted coordination.
- Notion: knowledge, documents, project context, flexible workspace.
- Salesforce Agentforce: governed agents and business-system integration.
- Focused automation tools: triggers/actions, connector depth, retries, operational visibility.

Compare jobs completed, setup effort, integration depth, reliability, cost, human approval, and measurable outcome. Do not copy breadth or branding.

## Evidence capture

For each signal record URL, access/publication dates, source type, pain statement, persona, frequency and impact (observed vs inferred), workaround, buying intent, counter-evidence, confidence, candidate HIO workflow, and cheapest validation experiment.

## Scoring (0–5)

Score pain severity/frequency, current workaround cost, evidence quality, differentiation, delivery feasibility, permitted reachability, recurrence potential, and inverse legal/privacy/operational risk. Version weights. A score prioritizes; it does not prove demand.

## Validation ladder

1. Public signal: hypothesis only.
2. Repeated independent signals: test recurrence.
3. Workflow observation/interview: map steps, time, failure, decision criteria.
4. Prototype: measure completion, time saved, error rate, trust.
5. Pilot: measure repeated use and support.
6. Payment: measure real revenue and fulfillment cost.
7. Retention: verify repeat use and continuing value.

Avoid spam, unauthorized scraping, unsolicited automated outreach, or bypassing platform access controls.
