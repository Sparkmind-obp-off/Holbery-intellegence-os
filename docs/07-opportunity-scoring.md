# 07 — Opportunity Scoring Framework

## Purpose
Provide a transparent ranking aid for deciding what to investigate next. Scores are not objective truth, market-size estimates, or automatic launch decisions.

## Proposed dimensions (0–5)
| Dimension | Weight | Guiding question |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem severity | 15% | Does the problem cause meaningful loss, risk, or friction? |
| Recurrence / frequency | 10% | Does it recur for the same segment or workflow? |
| Commercial intent | 20% | Is there evidence of spend, buying intent, or paid alternatives? |
| Payer clarity | 10% | Can we identify who controls budget and pays? |
| Reachability | 10% | Can we reach qualified buyers through permitted channels? |
| Solution feasibility | 10% | Can HOLBERY deliver a credible minimum solution? |
| Economics | 10% | Could price, margin, support, and acquisition cost work? |
| Strategic fit / compounding | 10% | Does it strengthen reusable capability or portfolio assets? |
| Evidence quality / independence | 5% | Are the supporting records direct, diverse, and attributable? |

A weighted score may be calculated as the weighted mean of rated dimensions. Report coverage alongside the score; do not impute missing dimensions as 5. During early discovery, use a provisional score with explicit unknowns.

## Mandatory penalties / flags
- Strong counter-evidence: lower score or require more research.
- Single-source dependence: flag and cap confidence.
- Duplicated/reposted signals: count independent origin, not raw posts.
- Unclear payer or low urgency: block promotion until resolved.
- Inaccessible audience or prohibited channel: mark distribution risk.
- Delivery/legal/privacy risk: require review or reject.
- High cost or uncertain data access: require a cheap feasibility spike.
- Model-generated evidence without source records: invalid as primary support.

## Separate axes
Do not collapse these into one number:
1. Opportunity attractiveness.
2. Evidence confidence.
3. Data coverage.
4. Execution readiness.
5. Policy/legal risk.
6. Unit-economics confidence.

## Recommendation states
- **RESEARCH:** promising but critical unknowns remain.
- **HOLD:** insufficient evidence, poor timing, or access issue.
- **TEST CANDIDATE:** meets minimum evidence threshold; gate review required.
- **APPROVED TEST:** human-approved bounded experiment.
- **REJECT:** weak economics, no payer, poor fit, or material constraints.
- **ACTIVE / ITERATE / STOP:** post-test states based on pre-registered thresholds.

## Calibration
Version the weights and record why they change. Compare predicted scores with later outcomes. Avoid tuning the model to favor an already-preferred idea. The owner may override a recommendation but should record rationale.
